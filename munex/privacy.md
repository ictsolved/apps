# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how **ICT Solved!** ("we", "us", "our") handles your information when you use the **Munex** financial decision engine app ("App").

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account Data
When you register, we collect:
- **Email address** — used to identify your account and send transactional emails
- **Full name** — displayed in the app (optional)
- **Phone number** — optional, stored for your profile

When you sign in with **Google**, we receive your Google email address and display name from Firebase Authentication to create or link your account.

### 1.2 Financial Data
All financial information you enter is stored on our servers, linked to your account:
- Income entries and sources
- Expense records and categories
- Recurring transaction schedules
- Budget limits per category
- Savings goals and progress
- Emergency fund targets
- Cash flow forecasts and risk scores generated from your data
- Workspace settings and allocation percentages

This data is used solely to provide the App's features and is never shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

### 1.3 Session & Security Data
We log your **IP address** and **device user-agent string** when you sign in, and store them alongside your session token. This is used exclusively for security purposes (detecting suspicious sign-ins, session management). Logs are cleared when you log out or delete your account.

### 1.4 Push Notification Token
If you enable push notifications, we store your device's **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token**. This token is used only to deliver in-app alerts (budget warnings, risk changes, spending limits). It is deleted when you log out or delete your account.

### 1.5 Error Tracking
We use **Sentry** (Functional Software, Inc.) to capture crash reports and error events. These reports contain:
- Stack traces and error details
- App version and build
- Device model and OS version

No financial data or personally identifiable information is intentionally included in error reports.

### 1.6 Subscription & Billing
If you subscribe to Munex Pro:
- On **Android**, purchases are processed by **Google Play Billing** (Google LLC). We receive a purchase token to verify your subscription status.
- On other platforms, purchases may be processed by **Stripe** (Stripe, Inc.). Stripe stores your payment details; we store only your Stripe customer ID and subscription status.

We never store raw payment card information.

### 1.7 Transactional Email
We send transactional emails only:
- Email verification codes at registration
- Password reset links
- Monthly financial summary reports (if you have an account)
- Email change confirmation codes

We do not send marketing emails.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

| Purpose | Data Used |
|---|---|
| Core app functionality | Financial data (income, expenses, budgets, goals) |
| Account identification | Email, full name |
| Security & session management | IP address, user agent, session tokens |
| Cash flow forecasting & risk scoring | Financial data (processed server-side) |
| Push notifications | FCM device token |
| Subscription management | Google Play purchase token or Stripe customer ID |
| Bug fixing & stability | Sentry error reports |
| Transactional emails | Email address |

We do **not** sell, rent, or share your personal or financial data with any third party for marketing or advertising purposes.

## 4. Data Retention

- **Account and financial data** is retained for as long as your account is active. You can permanently delete everything at any time — see [Account & Data Deletion](/apps/munex/delete-account).
- **Session data** (IP, user agent, tokens) is deleted when the session expires, when you log out, or when you delete your account.
- **FCM tokens** are deleted when you log out or delete your account.
- **Sentry error data** is retained per [Sentry's data retention policy](https://sentry.io/privacy).
- **Stripe billing data** is retained per [Stripe's privacy policy](https://stripe.com/privacy). Deleting your Munex account does not automatically delete your Stripe customer record; contact us if you need complete removal.
- **Google Play Billing data** is managed by Google per their policies.

---

## 5. Data Security

Your data is stored on encrypted servers. Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms and never stored in plain text. Access tokens are short-lived and refresh tokens are rotated on use.

## 6. Your Rights

You may at any time:
- **Delete your account and all financial data** — see our [Account & Data Deletion page](/apps/munex/delete-account) for step-by-step instructions
- **Update your profile** (name, phone) via Settings in the app
- **Change or reset your password** via Settings or the Forgot Password flow
- **Revoke Google Sign-In** by unlinking from your Google account settings
- **Contact us** for any data-related request at [ictsimplified@gmail.com](mailto:ictsimplified@gmail.com)
