# Delete Account & Data

**App:** Munex – Financial Decision Engine  
**Developer:** ICT Solved!

---

## Delete Your Account In-App

You can permanently delete your account and all financial data directly inside the app.

**Steps:**

1. Open the **Munex** app
2. Tap **More** in the bottom navigation bar
3. Tap **Profile**
4. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete Account**
5. Confirm when prompted

**What gets permanently deleted:**
- Your account (email, name, phone)
- All income entries and sources
- All expense records and categories
- All recurring transaction schedules
- All budget limits and savings goals
- All emergency fund records
- All cash flow forecasts and risk scores
- All workspace data and settings
- All active sessions and push notification tokens

**This action is irreversible.** Your data cannot be recovered after deletion.

---

## What Is Not Deleted In-App

| Data | Deleted In-App | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Account & financial data | ✅ Immediately | All data removed from our servers |
| Active sessions & FCM token | ✅ Immediately | Signed out on all devices |
| Sentry error reports (anonymous) | — | Retained per [Sentry's policy](https://sentry.io/privacy) |
| Stripe billing records | — | Retained by Stripe per their policy |
| Google Play purchase records | — | Managed by Google |
