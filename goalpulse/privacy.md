# Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how **ICT Solved!** ("we", "us", "our") handles your information when you use the **GoalPulse** FIFA World Cup 2026 app ("App").

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account & Profile Data
When you sign in with **Google Sign-In**, we receive from Google and store on our servers:
- Your **display name** and **profile photo URL** (used to show your profile in-app)
- A unique **Firebase user ID** (used to associate your data with your account)

We do **not** store your email address.

### 1.2 Data You Create (Stored on Our Servers)
The following data you create in the App is stored on **our servers** and linked to your account:
- **Match predictions** — your predicted scores for each match
- **Prediction points** — points calculated after each match based on accuracy
- **Leaderboard rankings** — your rank snapshots after each match
- **Quiz answers** — your responses to halftime quiz questions
- **Match watchlist** — matches you have bookmarked for notifications

### 1.3 Locally Stored Data
App settings and preferences (such as notification preferences) are stored **locally on your device** using on-device storage and are never sent to our servers.

### 1.4 Analytics
We use **Firebase Analytics** (Google LLC) to collect anonymous usage data such as:
- Which features are used (e.g., screens viewed, predictions submitted)
- Session counts and retention metrics
- Device type and OS version (no personal identifiers)

This data is aggregated and anonymous. Analytics is **disabled in debug builds**.

### 1.5 Crash Reporting
We use **Firebase Crashlytics** (Google LLC) to collect crash reports that help us fix bugs. Crash reports contain:
- Stack traces and error details
- Device model and OS version
- App version

No personally identifiable information is included. Crashlytics is **disabled in debug builds**.

### 1.6 Push Notifications
If you enable match notifications, we use **Firebase Cloud Messaging** (Google LLC) to deliver push alerts (kick-off, goals, final whistle). A push token is generated on your device and stored in Firebase solely for delivering notifications. This token is not linked to your identity and can be reset by disabling and re-enabling notifications.

### 1.7 Remote Configuration
We use **Firebase Remote Config** (Google LLC) to adjust App settings (such as ad refresh rates) without a full app update. No personal data is collected via Remote Config.

### 1.8 Advertising
The App displays ads served by **Google AdMob** (Google LLC). AdMob may collect your advertising ID and use it to show relevant ads. You can opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings under **Google > Ads**.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

| Purpose | Data Used |
|---|---|
| Account identification | Firebase user ID, display name, photo URL |
| Match predictions & scoring | Server-stored predictions and points |
| Leaderboard | Server-stored ranking snapshots |
| Halftime quiz | Server-stored quiz answers |
| Match watchlist | Server-stored watchlist entries |
| App configuration | Firebase Remote Config (no personal data) |
| Bug fixing | Crashlytics crash reports |
| Feature improvement | Firebase Analytics events |
| Match notifications | Firebase Cloud Messaging push token |
| Ad delivery | AdMob advertising ID |

We do **not** sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party for marketing purposes.

## 4. Data Retention

- **Account and server data** (predictions, points, leaderboard, quiz answers, watchlist) is retained on our servers for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account removes all of this data permanently.
- **Local settings** remain on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its data in your device settings.
- **Push token** is retained in Firebase Cloud Messaging for as long as notifications are enabled.
- **Analytics and crash data** is retained by Google per their standard retention policies.
- **AdMob advertising data** is handled per Google's advertising policies.

## 5. Your Rights

You may at any time:
- **Delete your account and all associated data** — via **Fan Zone → My Zone → Delete account** in the App, or see our [Account Deletion page](/apps/goalpulse/delete-account)
- **Disable push notifications** in the App's notification settings or your device settings
- **Opt out of personalized ads** via your device's ad settings (Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization)
- **Contact us** with any data-related request at [ictsimplified@gmail.com](mailto:ictsimplified@gmail.com)
