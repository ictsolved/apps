# Delete Account & Data

**App:** GoalPulse – World Cup 2026  
**Developer:** ICT Solved!

---

## Delete Your Account (In-App)

You can delete your GoalPulse account and all associated data directly from the app at any time.

**Steps:**

1. Open the **GoalPulse** app
2. Tap **Fan Zone** in the bottom navigation
3. Go to the **My Zone** tab
4. Tap **Delete account**
5. Confirm when prompted

**What gets permanently deleted:**
- Your GoalPulse profile (display name, photo URL)
- All your match predictions and points
- Your leaderboard history
- All your quiz answers
- Your match watchlist

Your Google account is not affected — only your GoalPulse data and sign-in association are removed.

## Delete via Email

If you are unable to access the app, you can request deletion by email:

📧 [ictsimplified@gmail.com](mailto:ictsimplified@gmail.com?subject=Account%20Deletion%20Request%20–%20GoalPulse)

Please include **"Account Deletion Request – GoalPulse"** in the subject line. We will process your request within **7 days** and confirm by email.

---

## Disable Push Notifications

To stop receiving match notifications without deleting your account:

1. Open the **GoalPulse** app
2. Go to **Settings → Notifications**
3. Disable match alerts

Or revoke notification permission in your device settings under **Apps → GoalPulse → Notifications**.
